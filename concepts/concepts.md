## How to Use This Vault

1. **Study → capture to inbox** — dump raw notes in `inbox/`
2. **Weekly cleanup** — distribute to `concepts/`, create cards & diagrams
3. **Daily (10 min)** — open Obsidian, click flashcard bolt → syncs to Anki
4. **Weekly (30 min)** — blank-page test: redraw a diagram from memory, then diff against the Excalidraw file
5. **End of phase** — Feynman test: record yourself explaining core ideas
