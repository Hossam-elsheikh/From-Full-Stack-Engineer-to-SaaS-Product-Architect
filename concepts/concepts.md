## How this vault works now

1. **Study → paste raw notes** into the phase's `_inbox.md` — one capture point per phase, no top-level inbox
2. **Ask Claude to distribute** — notes get written here *and* merged into the phase artifact, republished at the same URL
3. **Review in the artifacts** — the phase sheet to read and self-test, the Build Bench and Year One to practise
4. **Mark the Atlas** at the end of each phase

Folder names match the plan's phase names (Rev B): `phase-0-orientation`, `phase-1-architecture`, `phase-2-data`, `phase-3-saas-mechanics`, `phase-4-delivery-cloud`, `phase-5-operate-maintain`, `phase-6-scale`, `phase-7-security`, `capstone`.
